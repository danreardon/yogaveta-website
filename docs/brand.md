# Yogaveta brand notes

Working notes for the site. Olivia's decisions override anything here.

## Fixed
- The logo and wordmark, as Olivia has them. Original files still needed (SVG, AI or PDF);
  the site uses a set-type stand-in, "yogaveta" in Cormorant Garamond, until they arrive.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond (display, light, italic for the second half of a heading),
  DM Sans (body, light).

## Colour (direction E, Nov 2026; awaiting Olivia's sign-off)
Roughly 70 / 20 / 10:
- Ground (most of the site): linen #F4F1EC, stone #DCD5CA / #CFC6B9, sand #E7E1D8,
  ink #2B2723, taupe #8A7F74.
- Depth (photo tints, bands): sage #A8B49C, sage tint #E3E6DB, forest #2F4A3A.
- Action only (buttons, booking): burgundy #6E2B35.
- The + mark: muted gold #8A6A3A.
Her original identity was sage, forest, gold and cream. Her Oct 2026 brief asked for
"neutral, a touch of burgundy". This palette is the combination of the two.

## The + mark
The brand device. Used in edition names ("yogaveta + clay"), as the separator in dates and
places ("Sun 29 Nov + Little Aston"), on the stamp card and the wax seal.

## Section names (her voice, to confirm)
This season (upcoming) · yogaveta + (the series) · The shop · Made with (partners) ·
Kind words (testimonials) · Notes from the mat (newsletter).

## Feel
Quiet luxury, minimal, editorial, unhurried. Motion is slow (the hero film, gentle fades);
nothing bounces. Influence: amare-bride.com for structure (one repeated action, named
sections, trunk shows as campaigns, shop on the homepage), not for its look.

## Photography (the biggest gap)
One planned shoot day: soft daylight, one consistent grade, a shot list covering the
8 to 12 second hero loop (breath, hands, spine, fabric; graceful, not suggestive), portraits,
hands and textures, events in progress. Hero film under about 3 MB, no sound, with a
still poster image.
